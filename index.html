About EONIX AI

EONIX is an executive-grade AI interface designed for planning, analysis, writing, image workflows, decision support, and operational execution. It is built around a focused command workspace that keeps conversations, context, modes, and tools visible without making the screen feel crowded.

The system includes multiple working modes: Prime for balanced reasoning, Swift for faster responses, DeepCore for research and logic, Oracle for strategy, Vision for image analysis, and Forge for image generation. These modes help users choose the right response style for the task at hand.

Core Capabilities

  • Structured problem solving, planning, and executive decision support.
  • Conversation history with searchable local sessions and export support.
  • Voice input, image attachment, and image generation entry points.
  • Theme, accent, density, and message scale controls for a personalized workspace.

Design Philosophy

EONIX favors a premium operational layout: compact controls, clear hierarchy, persistent context, and reduced duplicate actions. The interface is meant to feel calm, fast, and ready for repeated daily use.
